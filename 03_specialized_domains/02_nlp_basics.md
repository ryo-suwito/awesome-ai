# Natural Language Processing (NLP)

Computers don't understand words. They understand vectors.

## Pre-Deep Learning
*   **Bag of Words**: Count word frequency. Ignores order.
*   **TF-IDF**: Weighs words by importance (rare words have higher weight).

## Embeddings
The breakthrough. Representing words in a continuous vector space where similar words are close together.
*   **Word2Vec**: "King - Man + Woman = Queen".
*   **GloVe**: Global Vectors for Word Representation.

These paved the way for Transformers.
